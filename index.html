DIAMOND JUBILEE LEGACY · 1966–2026

Apostle Kehinde Olaoluwa Oladapo

Born on September 18, 1966, at Ibadan Street West, Ebute Metta, Lagos, to the late David Oladapo Fatukasi and the late Mrs Florence Olawunmi Fatukasi (née Oginni), Kehinde Olaoluwa Oladapo marks his sixtieth year with a legacy defined by missionary vision, indigenous leadership development, discipleship and institution-building. His journey reflects a sustained commitment to the Great Commission: reaching communities, raising indigenous workers, strengthening the Church and preparing successive generations for Christian service.

Raised among the Awori people of Itire, Surulere, Lagos, Oladapo developed an early appreciation of the relationship between culture, community and evangelism. This understanding informed his outreach among the Awori of Igando and his subsequent field evangelism during the 1991–1992 National Youth Service Corps programme in Oyi Local Government Area of the then Anambra State. Working from Nteje, he ministered in Nando, Igbariam, Ekulobia and neighbouring communities.

His missionary formation continued at World Outreach School of Missions, Onireke, Ibadan, between 1995 and 1996. On October 4, 1996, he relocated to Efon Alaaye, Ekiti State, where he established a training centre dedicated to raising indigenous local missionaries using Yoruba as a principal language of instruction.

This vision found broader institutional expression through Household of Missions Inc. Beyond Nigeria, his international ministry engagements have included the Republic of Benin, Togo, Ghana, Côte d’Ivoire, Canada and the United States.

A husband of one wife and father of four children—two daughters and two sons—Oladapo celebrates sixty years of life and thirty-two years of missionary leadership with gratitude to God.
